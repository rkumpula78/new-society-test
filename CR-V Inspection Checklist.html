6 · Test Drive20+ minutes, include highway

⛔ Feel discrete gear shifts
Clear 1-2, 2-3 shifts = 5AT = flat-towable. Continuous droning rev = CVT = 2015+ = WALK.
Shift quality
No rev flare between gears, no slip, no harsh 2-3, prompt downshifts.
Steering at 65 mph
Hands light on wheel — pull/wander was a known complaint. Alignment ($100) or crash damage (walk).
Brake test 60→10
Pulsation = warped rotors. Pull to one side = caliper.

Speed-dependent hum that changes with gentle swerves.
Engine mounts
Shudder/clunk on throttle tip-in and shifting to reverse — common at 140k.
Verify “new struts”
Controlled ride, no clunks. Clean strut bodies in wheel wells. Ask for receipt.
